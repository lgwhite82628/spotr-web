SPOTR Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to report something? Email spotrapp@protonmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Why didn't my car get identified? Make sure the whole car is in the photo and well lit. If SPOTR isn't sure, it shows a few options to pick from, or you can send it to Community ID for other spotters to name.

Why does SPOTR need my location? Points depend on how rare a car is where you saw it. Your exact location is never shown to anyone; others see only a rough area. See the privacy policy.

Is there a limit? Each person can identify up to 10 cars a day.

How do I report someone or something? Use the ••• menu on any spot, comment or profile and choose Report. You can also block people there.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, spots and photos from our servers.
