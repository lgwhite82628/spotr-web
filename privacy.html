SPOTR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

SPOTR is a car-spotting game: you photograph cars, the app identifies them and scores them by how rare they are near you, and you can share spots with friends. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account: the username and display name you choose, and optionally a profile photo, bio and favourite car maker. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a private identifier (and your name only if you share it). We don't ask for a password, phone number or email address.
  • Car photos you take: before a photo leaves your phone, SPOTR blurs licence plates and faces it detects and removes all hidden metadata (including GPS).
  • Location of each spot: needed to score how rare the car is where you saw it. Your exact location is stored privately and never shown to anyone. Other people see only a rough area (about a neighbourhood, or the city name), and people who aren't your friends see a spot only after 6 hours.
  • Activity in the app: your spots, points, reactions, comments, friends, crews and reports, so the game works.
  • Anonymous usage and crash data (if "Share anonymous usage" is on): a fixed list of events such as "spot captured", with no location, photo, spot or account identifier, and an install identifier that changes every 30 days. Crash reports come from Apple's MetricKit. You can turn this off in Settings.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, storage and servers (United States).
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives each blurred photo to identify the car. Anthropic does not use data sent through its API to train its models by default.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple or push notifications.

We don't sell your data, show third-party ads, or use tracking SDKs.

What other people can see

Your username, display name, profile photo, level and your spots (car, photo, rough area, points), unless you make your profile private, in which case only friends see your spots. You can block anyone; blocking is two-way.

Your choices

  • Delete a spot from its menu, or delete your account in Settings → Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, spots, photos and activity from our servers.
  • Download your data in Settings → Account → Export my data.
  • Turn off location or camera access at any time in iPhone Settings; spotting needs both.

Children

SPOTR is for people aged 13 and over. The app checks age before an account is made, using Apple's age range (which shares only whether you're 13 or older) or a birth date that is used once and never stored. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if anything changes and note the date above. Questions or requests: see the support page.
